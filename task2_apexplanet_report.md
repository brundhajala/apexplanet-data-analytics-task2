# Task 2: Exploratory Data Analysis and Business Intelligence

## Objective

The objective of this task was to analyze the official ApexPlanet cleaned sales dataset, answer business questions using SQL, and create a static sales dashboard.

## Dataset Summary

- Total orders: 992
- Total sales: INR 138,104,311.37
- Average order value: INR 139,218.06

## Key Insights

- Electronics generated the highest total sales, at INR 50,602,948.05.
- Laptop was the top product by total sales, at INR 25,301,994.63.
- Patna was the highest-performing city, generating INR 20,685,570.55 in sales.
- The 36-45 age group generated the highest sales, at INR 31,392,231.83.
- Male customers generated INR 71,657,355.44 in sales, while female customers generated INR 66,446,955.93.
- March 2025 recorded the highest monthly sales, at INR 13,051,317.45.

## SQL Business Questions Answered

1. What is the monthly sales trend?
2. Which five products generate the highest sales?
3. Which category generates the highest sales?
4. Which city generates the highest sales?
5. Which age group generates the highest sales?
6. How do sales differ by gender?
7. Which customers have the highest total sales?

## Dashboard Contents

The static dashboard includes:

- Total orders
- Total sales
- Average order value
- Top city
- Monthly sales trend
- Sales by category
- Sales by city
- Sales by age group

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize Electronics and Laptop inventory because they generate the highest sales.
- Study the factors behind Patna’s strong sales performance and apply suitable strategies in other cities.
- Target the 36-45 age group with tailored promotions and product recommendations.
- Monitor monthly sales trends to identify seasonal demand changes.
- Review customer-level sales data to identify high-value customers and potential loyalty opportunities.
